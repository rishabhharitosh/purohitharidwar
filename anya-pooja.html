FAQ

Anya Pooja – Frequently Asked Questions

Kya aap Haridwar ke bahar bhi pooja karwate hain?
Kuch pooja jaise Griha Pravesh ya Satyanarayan Katha aapke sthan par bhi karwayi ja sakti hain — booking ke samay location bata dein.
Rudrabhishek kab karna shubh mana jata hai?
Somvar, Pradosh aur Mahashivratri ke din Rudrabhishek karna vishesh shubh mana jata hai, halaanki yeh kisi bhi shubh din kiya ja sakta hai.
Navgrah Shanti Pooja kis liye karwate hain?
Kundli me graha dosh ya pratikool graha sthiti ko shant karne ke liye Navgrah Shanti Pooja karwayi jati hai.
Kya alag-alag pooja ke liye alag Purohit chahiye?
Nahi, hamare Tirth Purohit sabhi vaidik pooja aur sanskar shastriya vidhi se karwane me saksham hain.
Pooja ki booking kitne din pehle karni chahiye?
Muhurat aur samagri ki vyavastha ke liye kam se kam 3-5 din pehle booking karna behtar rehta hai.
