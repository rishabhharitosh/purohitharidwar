Pind Daan Kya Hai Aur Kyu Zaroori Hai

Pind Daan kya hai?

Pind Daan ek vaidik karm hai jo apne pitron (purkhon) ki atma ki shanti ke liye kiya jata hai. Ismein pind, til aur jal ke madhyam se purkhon ko tarpan diya jata hai.

Pind Daan Haridwar me hi kyu?

Haridwar pavitra tirth sthalon me se ek hai jahan Maa Ganga swayam bahti hai. Yahan Pind Daan karne se pitron ko sadgati aur moksh milta hai — yahi maanyata peedhiyon se chali aa rahi hai.

Pind Daan kab karte hain?

Pitru Paksha, Amavasya, Shraddh ke din aur kisi vyakti ke dehant ke baad nirdharit tithi par Haridwar me Pind Daan kiya jata hai. Sahi tithi ke liye Purohit se salah lena uchit rehta hai.
