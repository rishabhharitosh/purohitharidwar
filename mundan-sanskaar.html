Mundan Sanskar Kya Hai Aur Kyu Zaroori Hai

Mundan Sanskar kya hai?

Mundan Sanskar hindu dharm ke solah sanskaron me se ek hai, jisme bachche ke janam ke baad pehli baar shastriya vidhi se baal utarwaye jaate hain. Yeh sanskar bachche ke swasthya aur mangal kamna ke liye kiya jata hai.

Haridwar me Mundan Sanskar kyu?

Maa Ganga ke pavitra tat par Mundan Sanskar karne se bachche ko dirghayu, arogya aur purkhon ka aashirwad milta hai — yahi maanyata sadiyon se chali aa rahi hai.

Mundan Sanskar kab karte hain?

Aam taur par bachche ke pehle, teesre ya paanchve varsh me, panchang ke anusaar shubh muhurat dekh kar Mundan Sanskar karwaya jata hai. Sahi muhurat ke liye Purohit se salah lena uchit rehta hai.
